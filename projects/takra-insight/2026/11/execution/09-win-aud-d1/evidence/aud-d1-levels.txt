TC-AUD-D.1 — level check of the simulated live before it was used
=================================================================

Built by scripts/build-aud-d1.ps1 from real Thai host speech already analysed on this machine.
Timeline (330 s total, video continuous throughout):

    0 -  90 s   real Thai speech
   90 - 135 s   DIGITAL SILENCE (all zero samples) <- the "mic muted" window
  135 - 330 s   speech again, so the episode can recover

The engine's threshold for "silence" is -60 dBFS (SILENCE_DBFS, wall_alerts.py). Measured with
the app's own bundled ffmpeg (resources/ffmpeg/ffmpeg.exe, pinned gyan 8.1.1) reading the FINISHED
FLV back through the AAC decoder, so these are the levels the engine's audio tap actually sees —
not the levels of the source wav before encoding.

  stretch            RMS level
  -----------------  -------------------------------
  speech  60- 70 s   -18.976897 dBFS   (well above the threshold)
  silence 100-130 s  -inf              (true digital zero, survives AAC)
  speech  150-160 s  -13.901473 dBFS   (well above the threshold)

Why this matters: AAC is lossy, so "I wrote zeros into the source" is not evidence that the
decoder hands zeros to the engine. Reading the level back out of the built FLV is. -inf across a
30 s probe inside the window confirms the silence rule is being fed what the case needs, and the
two speech probes confirm the surrounding audio could never be mistaken for silence.

No LHM_WALL_* override was set in the run, so the boundary under test is the shipped 20 s.
